TIER 1 PILOT • 19 INSTRUCTIONAL HOURS

IT Support & Help Desk
Foundations

A five-part draft for adult learners building confidence with first-line support. Learners practice with fictional tickets and training examples—never real customer accounts, passwords, or production systems.

01 • 4 HOURS

Tier 1 role and triage

Clarify the issue, identify impact and urgency, follow a safe troubleshooting sequence, and know when to stop or escalate.

02 • 4 HOURS

Devices, operating systems, and tickets

Practice basic Windows 11 navigation, macOS comparisons, peripherals, user-safe checks, and clear ticket notes.

03 • 4 HOURS

Connectivity and workplace tools

Explain Wi-Fi, Ethernet, IP, DNS, and service status; distinguish device, account, and broader service issues.

04 • 4 HOURS

Security, communication, and AI

Use MFA and least privilege, recognize phishing, protect private information, and review AI-drafted text using fictional examples.

05 • 3 HOURS

Integrated support capstone

Triage a fictional multi-symptom issue, document safe checks, update the user, and prepare an actionable escalation.

A STACKABLE LEARNING MAP

Foundations first.
Then specialize.

Each next step should have its own scope, prerequisites, lab hours, instructor plan, and learner assessment.

IT support and cybersecurity

IT foundations → CompTIA A+ preparation → Network+ preparation → Security+ preparation. This progression can support readiness for help desk, desktop support, junior network support, and later security-support roles. Certification exams and credentials are awarded by CompTIA, not by DivineGuard.

Software engineering

A separate pathway can build HTML/CSS, JavaScript or Python, Git/GitHub, SQL, REST APIs, testing, and portfolio projects toward QA, junior developer, and full-stack roles. It is not included in the 19-hour Tier 1 pilot.

Responsible AI at work

Learners practice using sponsor-approved AI tools for fictional ticket summaries and drafts, checking every result, removing sensitive information, and getting human approval before use.
